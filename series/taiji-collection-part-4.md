# Taiji Collection (part 4) – Flower & Gemstone Coin Taiji Motif Art

In this series, the two fish eyes of the Taiji diagram are replaced by **flower coins** and **gemstone coins** – each blooming or sparkling with a square hole (or square mark) at the center – a signature of the coin.

**Flower coins** (lily, rose, carnation, plum blossom, cherry blossom, peony, sunflower, golden jasmine, daisy) carry the soft, organic energy of nature.  
**Gemstone coins** feature eight distinct varieties of precious stones – ruby, sapphire, emerald, diamond, amethyst, citrine, opal, and jade – each placed at the fish eye position, carrying the square hole (or square mark) that represents the coin.

Together, they speak the language of yin and yang:  
- **Light coins (yang)** – active, visible, circulating wealth.  
- **Dark coins (yin)** – receptive, hidden, stored capital.

This is not a simple decoration. It is a meditation on balance – between bloom and gem, between softness and hardness, between growth and preservation.

Perfect for **architectural ceilings**, **interior wall art**, **fabric prints**, **stationery**, **apparel**, **home decor**, and **gift products** seeking a refined yet meaningful touch.

Where flowers and diamonds meet the void, and Taiji learns to shine.
