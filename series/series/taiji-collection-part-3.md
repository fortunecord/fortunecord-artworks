# Taiji Collection (part 3) – Fruit Coin Taiji

Instead of traditional fish eyes, **fruit coins** now sit at the heart of Taiji — apple, strawberry, mango, grape, each carrying the shape of a round coin with a soft, fruity character.  
White fruit coin (yang) symbolizes active, visible, circulating wealth.
Black fruit coin (yin) symbolizes receptive, hidden, stored capital.

This is not a mere replacement. It is a conversation: between yin and yang, between feng shui wealth and natural abundance, between the sweetness of fruit and the eternal cycle.
Perfect for architectural ceilings, interior decor, printed matter, apparel, and home accessories seeking harmony with a fresh, playful touch.
Where fruit meets the void, and Taiji learns to blossom.
