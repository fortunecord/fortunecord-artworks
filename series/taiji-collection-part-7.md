# Taiji Collection (Part 7) – Chain & Multi-Material Coin Taiji Art

This collection extends the Fortune Cord Taiji system into **chain-linked forms**, where the outer ring and spiral lines are rendered as double strands, chains, or cord loops — while the yin-yang fish eyes are replaced with **furry, floral, gem, and fruit coins**.  

Each piece explores a different combination of:
- **Connection type:** O-chain, O-ring, cross-chain, blockchain, single strand, double strand, spiral
- **Coin type:** Furry beasts, flowers, gemstones, fruits
- **Pose & view:** Sitting, crouching, side, back, front

A dialogue between soft and hard, organic and mechanical — all woven into the timeless rhythm of Taiji.
