# Big Dipper Collection (Part 2) – Star Coin Ceiling Art

The Plough turns around Polaris, marking seasons and nights. This collection continues the celestial journey with furry coin motifs — two-eared beasts, single-eared beasts, and earless creatures — each carrying a square pocket on the belly and a coiled, fluffy, or braided tail.

These are black-and-white line drawings, featuring front, back, and side views of each beast in sitting and crouching postures. The cord runs through every coin, transforming constellation nodes into soft, living characters.

Perfect for starlight headliners, car roof designs, architectural ceilings, fabric prints, and home decor.

---

### Series Details
- Coin types: Two-eared beast · Single-eared beast · Earless beast
- Tail styles: Coiled cord tail · Fluffy tail · Braided tail · Straight tail
- Poses: Sitting · Crouching
- Views: Front · Back · Side
