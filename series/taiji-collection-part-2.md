# Taiji Collection (part 2) - Round Coin Taiji Motif Art

Round coins replace the fish eyes of the Taiji diagram, with no cord elements — pure coin, pure circulation.
The light coin and the dark coin move in endless rotation, each containing the seed of the other. This is not a static symbol, but a continuous flow: visible wealth and invisible wealth, rising and receding, exchanging places in an unbroken cycle.
Pre-Heaven and Post-Heaven versions included.
Designed for architectural ceilings, decorative surface patterns, interior wall art, and print products.
