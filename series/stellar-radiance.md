# Stellar Radiance – Star Coin Pulse

The grammar is consistent: a coin at the center, rays radiating outward — each ray a cord or chain.
Not all stars burn steady. Some pulse. And in that pulse, there is a rhythm — a reminder that light, like wealth, comes in waves.
 
107 images, built from:
- 9 coin types (square hole, round hole, floral perforation, four-notched, four-radiating, and more)
- 8 cord types (straight, curved, braided, knotted, wavy, wrapped, spiral, figure-eight)
- 3 chain types (progressive, graduated, and spiral-chain hybrids)

Designed for automotive headliners, architectural ceilings, decorative surface patterns, interior wall art, fabric prints, gift packaging, or digital collectibles.
